### `offset_silhouette(document_path, page_index, shape_ids, offset, unit="pt", direction="inside", exclude_colors=None, color=None, target_layer=None, overprint_fill=None, keep_sources=True, name=None, corner="miter")`

- Inputs expand to their leaves (groups; effect groups and effect-generated objects only while sources are kept,
  since they are only copied). Skipped and listed: leaves matching `exclude_colors` (the shared color spec,
  matched by `find_shapes`' scanner, so fills incl. fountain stops, outlines and every text run count), hidden
  leaves, leaves without fill and outline. Refused: bitmaps, PowerClip contents and containers, master layers,
  and (when consuming) locked layers or effect-generated objects. Sources on locked layers are allowed while kept.
- Painted area per leaf: a fill-only shape gives its `DisplayCurve`. Outlines and text use a temporary
  `CopyToLayer` copy on the result layer: text is converted to curves, and the outline becomes an object with
  `Outline.ConvertToObject()` (stroke width, caps, joins, pen as drawn). CorelDRAW deletes an outline-only part
  once its outline is converted; the cleanup checks liveness before deleting.
- Union in memory with `Curve.WeldWith` (identical result to `Shape.Weld`: same subpaths and area on the "OBO" +
  rectangles probe). Offset with `Curve.Contour(offset, direction, square cap, corner, 15)`, which matched a
  contour effect on realistic text (area 5.09652 in² both, 10 subpaths kept) without creating effect objects.
- The result is created on the target layer, filled (default: the first participant's fill), outline removed,
  fill overprint set, named, and ordered behind the backmost participant on that layer.
- Verification: page StaticIDs after = before + result (− consumed sources), layer, fill identity, overprint
  flag, area (holes subtracted) and bounds, silhouette before the offset, offset achieved (mean = area change /
  perimeter; minimum per side of the bounds; problem if a side moved less than the offset), warnings when thin
  parts disappeared. Capped at 400 participants (see measurements).

### `combine_shapes(document_path, page_index, shape_ids, operation, keep_sources=False, target_layer=None, preserve_order=True)`

weld / trim (first ID cut by the others) / intersect (common area, cumulative) / combine / break_apart.
`preserve_order` keeps `weld_consecutive_shapes`' guarantees (one layer, top level, adjacent, identical fill
incl. `IsSame` spot and tint, outline, overprint; result between the same neighbors, checked on a fresh walk).
`keep_sources=true` works on copies (`CopyToLayer(target_layer)` or `Duplicate`). P12 fixed: results move to
`target_layer`. Read-back: result IDs, layer, area/perimeter/subpaths/bounds, paint; weld area in [largest
source, sum], bounds = union of sources; intersection ≤ smallest; trim ≤ target; combine/break_apart keep every
subpath. Rollback on any failure through `undoable`.

## CorelDRAW findings

1. `Curve.Area` / `DisplayCurve.Area` add every subpath: a 4×4 square with a 2×2 hole reads 20 (true 12). Holes
   must be found by nesting (`SubPath.IsPointInside`, which CQL cannot call).
2. `ShapeRange.CreateBoundary` fills holes (letter counters): "OBO" boundary 1.163 vs welded 1.765 (sum of
   subpaths). Not usable for an underbase. `Curve.SelfWeldClosedSubpaths` and `RemoveOverlaps` give wrong unions.
3. `Curve.WeldWith(other)` returns a new curve and leaves the receiver unchanged.
4. Contour corner enum (`cdrContourCornerType`): bevel 0, offset bevel 1, round 2, mitered bevel 3, mitered offset
   bevel 4 (CorelDRAW's default; end cap 2, miter limit 15). `CreateContour` returns None when the offset removes
   the shape.
5. An empty command group is its own undo step (two `Undo(1)` after two empty/failed groups did not undo the
   earlier edit; the third did). So `Undo(1)` after a failed group never undoes an earlier edit.
6. `CopyToLayer` copies a nested group child to the top level of another layer, works from locked layers, and
   writes into locked layers through COM. `Duplicate` also creates objects on locked layers. Tools must check.
7. `Outline.ConvertToObject()` returns the stroke as a filled curve and removes the outline; a shape without fill
   is deleted by CorelDRAW. `ConvertToCurves` keeps the StaticID. Text has no usable `DisplayCurve`; groups
   return None.
8. `Effect.Separate()` is generic: drop shadow → plain object (shadow group dissolved); 2-step contour → the
   contour group becomes an ordinary group with the same ID.

## Risks

- `Curve.WeldWith` on production art may crash CorelDRAW (incident 2). The tool always welds into a fresh curve
  (`CreateCurve` + `AppendCurve`) with display curves as arguments, which ran without a crash on screen_job and
  on the 1,870-leaf Good News group (until the timeout), but the root cause is unknown. Before merging, consider
  `Shape.Weld` on temporary copies (the proven T6a path) for the union, and test on Good News on a CorelDRAW
  instance that no one else is using.
- `Curve.Contour` and the contour effect agreed on realistic art, but differed in a degenerate case where the
  inset removes thin strokes (the effect kept a filled blob; `Curve.Contour` kept small pieces). Confirm with
  `render_plates(contain=…)`.
- Open paths with a fill are welded as given; not tested.
- Mixed-run text is converted as a whole and counts as participating if any run is painted, unless a run matches
  `exclude_colors` (then the whole text is skipped).
