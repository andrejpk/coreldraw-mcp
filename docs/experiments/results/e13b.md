# E13b: make E13 crash-safe and validated (`offset_silhouette`, `combine_shapes`, effects, duplicates, curves)

## Outcome: success (ready to merge)

No crash: CorelDRAW 2022 (one process, PID unchanged) ran every check, the sample copies and the Good News timing.
The shared-instance gate (`scripts/validation/e13b/gate.py`, `list_documents` through MCP) ran before every script
session; only documents under `C:\CorelMcpLab\runs\e13b` were ever open. Nothing on `Z:` was touched. All MCP
work; the only in-process sessions were the existing `build_accept.py`, `accept_failure.py` and `close_run_docs.py`
with the server stopped. `probe3.py` / `probe12.py` were not run; `Curve.WeldWith` was not called.

## Hypothesis

Replacing E13's in-memory geometry with shape-level operations on temporary copies (the path the pre-E13 T6a
tools used) removes the crash risk without losing E13's results, and MCP acceptance checks show the new tools are
correct, refuse what they should, and undo exactly.

## What changed

Tools: **81** (unchanged from E13; 85 on the integration branch before E13). Catalog (`catalog_size.py`):
**81 tools, 90,288 JSON chars, ≈22,572 tokens** (E13 89,979; integration 89,756 / 85 tools).

## Risks

- `ShapeRange.CopyToLayer` + `Combine` of bbox-disjoint fills is new (validated by area/subpath/hole equality
  with sequential welds on Good News and screen_job). Combining relies on CorelDRAW's even-odd fill; bounding boxes
  that touch are put in different sets.
- Throughput for outlined art is ~100 objects per call; larger selections fail cleanly (budget) and must be split,
  which yields several results (weld them afterwards with `combine_shapes`).
- Undo after the budget stop took ~11 s for ~300 commands; much more work before the stop would push the total
  closer to 60 s. The budget (35 s) leaves room for that.
- `combine_shapes` has no time budget; a weld of several hundred shapes could still reach the bridge limit.
- Effect groups are refused (capability narrower than E13 designed); separate effects first.
- `optimized()` changes app-wide flags for the duration of the call (restored in `finally`).
